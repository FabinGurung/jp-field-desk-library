08 / WORKFLOW AND NOTIFICATIONS

How a field entry reaches other websites

  1. Capture: update the existing company materials, daily-progress, attendance or measurement Sheet with its original project ID and source.
  2. Review: preserve original evidence and distinguish reported, verified, rejected or unknown entries.
  3. Publish: the responsible repository's authenticated GitHub Actions reader validates the source before producing public-safe JSON. A Sheet edit alone does not imply that every repo has refreshed.
  4. Consume: Site Operations may show accepted daily progress, Notifications may show explicitly approved action items, Scheduling uses approved resource quantities, and Cost Estimation computes quantities/rates/payments from its own controlled readers.
  5. Close: retain a record ID, original source, reviewer, revision and A7 version history. Do not delete cancelled or resolved history.

Currently, the Resources publisher directly reads its Project Resources catalogue and the separately authorized historical CLEAN Payment. Company inventory/attendance/progress Sheets are linked for entry, not yet admitted as live public projection feeds. Approval and authenticated-reader validation are required before automating those values to other sites.
